# AI Native iOS

A concept for iOS 27. The assistant moves into the dock, answers land on your home screen, and nothing sends, books or changes without your yes.

**Live case study and prototype:** https://indo1919.github.io/AI-Native-IOS/

The page walks through the whole project: research on iOS 26, version 1, a critique after iOS 27 shipped, and the five decisions behind version 2. It ends with a working prototype (nine flows, from quick answers to a booked table) and a short scripted film.

**Role:** Sole designer, both versions

**Tools:** Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop, Procreate, Vizcom, HTML

An independent design concept by Matias Indacochea, not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple. App icons and all content in the prototype are original placeholders.
